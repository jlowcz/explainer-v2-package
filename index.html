🚀 Quick Start

Generate your first interactive explainer in 3 steps.

Pick the version that matches your needs:

  • V3 - Animated phases + full interactivity

Step 2: Use with AI

Copy the prompt template and ask an AI:

"Using the V3 explainer format, explain [your topic]"

Step 3: Add Assets

Place the generated HTML alongside the assets/ and icons/ folders, then open in your browser!
